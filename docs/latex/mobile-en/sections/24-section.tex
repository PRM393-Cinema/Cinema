\section{Out of Scope}\label{sec:srs-24}
Exclude phone \texttt{CRUD} for movies, rooms, seat layouts, showtimes, accounts, snack menu, promotions or members; counter/\allowbreak{}cash sales, receipt print, operational/\allowbreak{}revenue dashboards and refund approve/\allowbreak{}reject/\allowbreak{}transfer recording. Those Dart screens are web-only despite sharing the repository.

No implemented \texttt{FCM} push or automatic bank-refund integration is claimed. Payroll, inventory and \texttt{AI} recommendations are not added. Existing concessions, promotions/\allowbreak{}voucher codes, membership/\allowbreak{}loyalty and reviews remain in scope where actually exposed by code.
